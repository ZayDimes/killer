📖 Rules
  • Goal: get rid of all your cards first. Last player holding cards loses.
  • Ranks: 3 low → 10, J, Q, K, A, 2 high. Suits: ♠ < ♣ < ♦ < ♥.
  • Suits: below 10, equal rank can go on equal rank. From 10 up, equal rank needs a higher suit.
  • First game: whoever has the 3♠ leads and must include it. After that, the last winner leads anything.
  • Plays: single, pair, triple, run (3+ in a row, no wrap, can end in a 2), suited run.
  • Follow the lead's type and length with something equal or higher, or pass. Passing doesn't knock you out of the round.
  • Suited runs: once a suited run is played, that round is suited runs only.
  • When everyone passes, the last player to play leads anything. If they're out, the lead goes left. Left is law.
  • Killer (four of a kind): beats anything, including bombs. Higher killer beats lower. Can be led.
  • Bomb (3+ consecutive pairs, triples, or quads): only goes on a play with a 2 in it (including a bomb with 2s). On a bomb without 2s: same type and length, equal or higher. Can't be led unless the last round ended on a 2.
  • 9 on a 6 = everyone says NICE.
  • 3 losses in a row = punishment. 🔥 3 wins in a row puts you in the Hall of Fame, but only in games with 4+ real people.
  • 🤖 CPU: the host can add up to 2 CPUs in the lobby (Easy, Medium or Hard), with any number of players. If someone new joins from the chair and that makes 3+ real people, the CPUs leave after that game and the 3♠ leads the next one. CPU wins never count for the Hall of Fame.
