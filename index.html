📖 Rules

Basics

  • Get rid of all your cards first. Last one holding cards loses.
  • Ranks: 3 (low) → 10, J, Q, K, A, 2 (high). Suits: ♠ < ♣ < ♦ < ♥.
  • Below 10, same rank can go on same rank. From 10 up, same rank needs a higher suit.

Starting

  • First game: the 3♠ leads and must be in the play. After that, the last winner leads.
  • 6+ players (CPUs count) uses two decks. With two decks, the exact same card can go on itself (10♥ on 10♥), and both 3♠ holders race to tap Claim 3♠. First tap leads.

Playing

  • Plays: single, pair, triple, run (3+ in a row, no wrap), suited run.
  • Match the lead's type and length, equal or higher, or pass. Passing doesn't knock you out of the round.
  • A suited run makes the rest of that round suited runs only.
  • Everyone passes → last player to play leads. If they're out, the lead goes left. Left is law.

Power plays

  • Killer (four of a kind): beats anything. Higher killer beats lower.
  • Bomb (3+ consecutive pairs, triples or quads): only goes on a play with a 2. On a bomb: same shape, equal or higher. Can only be led if the last round ended on a 2.

Extras

  • 9 on a 6 = NICE.
  • 3 losses in a row = punishment.
  • 3 wins in a row = Hall of Fame (4+ real people; CPU wins don't count).
  • 🤖 Host can add up to 2 CPUs (Easy, Medium, Hard). They leave once 3+ real people are playing.
